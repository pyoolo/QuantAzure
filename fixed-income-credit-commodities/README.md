# Section 2: Fixed Income, Credit & Commodities - Research Guide

## Overview

This section contains research and analysis materials focused on fixed income securities, credit instruments, and commodity markets. Materials include bond analysis, credit research, hedging strategies, and commodity market studies across diverse applications and market conditions.

## Subdirectories

### 💳 fixed-income/
Government and corporate bond analysis, yield curves, and debt strategy research.

**Type of content:**
- Government bond analysis and yield curve studies
- Corporate bond ratings and credit analysis
- Bond portfolio management strategies
- Duration and convexity analysis
- Fixed income derivatives and relative value analysis
- Floating rate note (FRN) and structured note analysis

**Naming convention:** `YYYYMMDD_Issuer_BondAnalysis.pdf` or `YYYYMMDD_YieldCurve_Analysis.pdf`

## Analytical Frameworks

### Fixed Income Analysis
- **Yield Analysis**: YTM, effective duration, convexity
- **Credit Analysis**: Leverage ratios, interest coverage, debt structure
- **Relative Value**: Spread analysis, curve positioning, sector rotation
- **Risk Metrics**: DV01, key rate durations, scenario analysis

### Credit Research
- **Quantitative Models**: Merton model, credit scoring models, machine learning classifiers
- **Qualitative Factors**: Industry dynamics, competitive position, management quality
- **Structural Analysis**: Capital structure, covenant packages, asset coverage
- **Macro Overlays**: Economic cycle, policy implications, contagion risk

### Commodity Analysis
- **Fundamental Analysis**: Supply/demand balance sheets, inventory levels, production costs
- **Technical Analysis**: Momentum, support/resistance, seasonal patterns
- **Macro Factors**: Dollar strength, real interest rates, risk appetite
- **Geopolitical**: Trade restrictions, sanctions, production disruptions

## Best Practices

✅ **Data Integrity**: Always verify historical data against multiple sources
✅ **Assumptions Documentation**: Clearly state model assumptions and sensitivities
✅ **Currency Considerations**: Note currency for international securities and commodities
✅ **Benchmark References**: Include benchmark curves and indices for context
✅ **Time Precision**: Include specific dates for time-sensitive analysis (especially spreads)
✅ **Covenant Documentation**: Maintain complete covenant language for credit analysis
✅ **Source Citations**: Link to external data sources (Bloomberg, Reuters, etc.)

❌ **Avoid**: Outdated yield curve data without historical context
❌ **Avoid**: Spread analysis without noting the reference benchmark
❌ **Avoid**: Commodity analysis without supply-demand balance sheet
❌ **Avoid**: Derivatives documentation without clearly stated assumptions

## Contributing Guidelines for Fixed Income & Commodities

When adding new materials:

1. **Classify clearly**: Is this fixed income, credit, derivatives, or commodities?
2. **Date everything**: Always include analysis/report date
3. **Document sources**: Link to data sources and methodologies
4. **Include context**: Provide benchmarks and comparative analysis where relevant
5. **Assumptions transparency**: State key assumptions and sensitivities
6. **Cross-reference**: Link to related materials in other sections
7. **Maintenance**: Update with current data for time-series analysis
