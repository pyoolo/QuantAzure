# Section 1: Equities - Research Guide

## Overview

This section contains comprehensive research materials focused on equity market analysis, stock valuation, and equity investment strategies. Materials include academic papers, case studies, market analysis, and practical frameworks for equity research.

## Subdirectories

### 📄 research-reports/
Detailed equity analysis reports and comprehensive market studies.

**Type of content:**
- Equity research reports (sell-side and buy-side)
- Industry analysis and market commentary
- Company-specific deep dives
- Market outlook reports and strategic analysis
- Performance attribution analysis

**Naming convention:** `YYYYMMDD_Company_ReportTitle.pdf` or `YYYYMMDD_Sector_Analysis.pdf`

---

### 📚 case-studies/
Real-world company case studies and valuation exercises.

**Type of content:**
- Complete company case studies (financials, strategy, valuation)
- M&A case studies and transaction analysis
- IPO and capital raise analysis
- Turnaround and restructuring case studies
- Investment thesis documents

**Naming convention:** `YYYYMMDD_CompanyName_CaseStudy.pdf` or `.xlsx`

---

### 🔍 sector-analysis/
Comprehensive sector-wide research and industry trends.

**Type of content:**
- Sector overview and macro trends
- Competitive dynamics and market structure analysis
- Regulatory and ESG considerations by sector
- Industry benchmarking and peer comparison
- Sector rotation and relative performance studies

**Naming convention:** `YYYYMMDD_SectorName_Analysis.pdf` or `YYYYMMDD_SectorName_Trends.pdf`

---

### 📊 data-sets/
Market data, financial statements, and research datasets.

**Type of content:**
- Historical stock price data (CSV or Excel)
- Company financial statements and ratios
- Industry benchmark datasets
- Economic indicators and macro data
- Performance data for backtesting

**Naming convention:** `SectorName_HistoricalData.csv` or `CompanyName_Financials_YYYY.xlsx`

**File formats:** `.csv`, `.xlsx`, `.parquet`, `.json`

---

### 🛠️ templates/
Reusable analysis templates and frameworks.

**Type of content:**
- Company analysis templates
- Valuation framework spreadsheets
- Investment thesis templates
- Due diligence checklists
- Model assumptions worksheets

**Naming convention:** `Template_TypeOfAnalysis.xlsx` or `Framework_Valuation.xlsx`

## Organization Tips

1. **By Time Period**: Recent research grouped chronologically
2. **By Sector**: Organize by GICS classification or industry
3. **By Company**: Alphabetical by company name within sector
4. **By Methodology**: Grouped by analysis type (valuation, growth, quality)
5. **By Project**: All materials related to a specific investment project

**Recommended approach:** Primary organization by sector, secondary by company or date

---

## Best Practices

✅ Always include the analysis date on documents
✅ Maintain source citations and links to original research
✅ Document key assumptions in analysis templates
✅ Keep both the analysis and supporting data together
✅ Use consistent formatting across related materials
✅ Update outdated analyses with current data when possible
✅ Include a summary page for lengthy reports

❌ Avoid using only company ticker symbols as filenames
❌ Don't store large raw datasets without documentation
❌ Avoid mixing multiple time periods in one file
❌ Don't forget to document methodology and key assumptions

## Contributing Guidelines for Equities

When adding new materials:

1. Choose the most specific subdirectory for your content
2. Follow the naming conventions for consistency
3. Include publication/analysis date in the filename
4. Add a one-sentence description of key findings
5. For complex analyses, include a summary document
6. Document all data sources and key assumptions
7. If multiple related documents exist, organize them in a subdirectory with an index
